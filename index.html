How to Use Surajit QR Code Helper

Follow these simple steps to generate, customize, and download high-performance QR codes for your business or personal projects.

1

Choose Your Content Type

Select from URL, vCard, Wi-Fi, PDF, or Social media tabs at the top of the generator box depending on what you want to share.

2

Customize & Add Logos

Personalize your QR code by picking custom foreground/background colors or uploading your brand logo directly to the center.

3

Save & Download Instantly

Click the Save or Download buttons to export your high-resolution QR code in PNG, JPG, or SVG vector formats.
